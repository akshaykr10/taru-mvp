Needs vs Wants

Week 6 · about 4 minutes · with Penny 🐿️

Some things we need — we can't live well without them. Other things we want — they're nice to have, but we'd be okay without them.

Needs are things like food, a place to live, clothes, and going to school.

Wants are things like a new toy, ice cream, or the latest video game. They make us happy, but they're a choice.

Money goes further when you cover your needs first, then choose your wants carefully — and maybe save or grow some too.

Rupee Cost Averaging 📊

Why investing a little every month beats trying to guess the best day.

When you invest the same amount every month, you buy more units when prices are low and fewer when they're high. Over time, your average price evens out — so you don't have to worry about timing it perfectly.
